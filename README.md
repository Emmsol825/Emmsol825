### 🎓 About me

Bachelor of Biomedical Engineering student at **Muhimbili University of Health and Allied Sciences (MUHAS)**, focused on bringing machine learning and AI into healthcare and biomedical problem-solving.

I work through full ML pipelines — data cleaning, feature engineering, model tuning, and evaluation — using Python and scikit-learn. My interest is in bridging biomedical engineering knowledge with data-driven approaches to health, so the models I build are grounded in the clinical problems they're meant to solve.
